Open this page in Chrome or Edge

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open the page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer.

Connect the switch

  1. Turn the switch on, then plug it into this computer with a USB cable. (Don't hold its power button while plugging it in – that starts it for programming.)
  2. Close the Arduino IDE if it's open – its Serial Monitor holds the switch's port.
  3. Click Connect and choose ESP32S3_DEV – TinyUSB CDC from the list.

Setup happens over the USB cable – no Wi-Fi. Recordings and typed words stay on this computer.

Custom key
A key or shortcut of your own, added to the Key choices – e.g. Win + H starts Windows dictation.
Bluetooth
The switch appears as ().
Keys go over USB when plugged into a computer, otherwise over Bluetooth (with No USB set to Bluetooth). To pair with a different computer or tablet, use Forget BT devices on the Settings tab.

Give a code a sound (e.g. "Bubbles!") and the switch says it as it sends – leave it out for silence. To learn a code: click Learn, point the remote at the end of the switch from 30cm–1m away and press its button within 8 seconds.

About this switch
Typed speech runs on this computer using Kokoro (Apache-2.0, via kokoro-js and Transformers.js) and Piper (MIT), eSpeak NG (GPL-3.0) and ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Piper voices: Cori – public domain recordings from LibriVox, model by Bryce Beattie; Alba – University of Edinburgh, CC BY 4.0; Southern English female and Northern English male – OpenSLR 83, CC BY-SA 4.0.
