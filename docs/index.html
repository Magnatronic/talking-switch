Talking Switch setup

Switch

Open this page in Chrome or Edge

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open setup.html in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a Windows, Mac, ChromeOS or Linux computer.

Connect the switch

  1. Plug the StickS3 into this computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Close the Arduino IDE Serial Monitor if it's open – only one program can use the switch at a time.
  3. Click Connect and choose the switch (usually "USB JTAG/serial debug unit" or "TalkingSwitch").

The switch is set up over the USB cable – no Wi-Fi. Recordings and typed words stay on this computer; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Slots: messages and IR codes

Each slot can hold a spoken message, an infrared (IR) remote code, or both. Record with this computer's microphone or a headset, upload any audio file, or type the words and let a voice say them – silence is trimmed and the level evened out automatically. Longest message: seconds.
