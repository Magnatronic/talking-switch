Open this page in Chrome or Edge

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open the page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer.

Connect the switch

  1. Turn the switch on, then plug it into this computer with a USB cable. (Don't hold its power button while plugging it in – that starts it for programming.)
  2. Close the Arduino IDE if it's open – its Serial Monitor holds the switch's port.
  3. Click Connect and choose ESP32S3_DEV – TinyUSB CDC from the list.

Setup happens over the USB cable – no Wi-Fi. Recordings and typed words stay on this computer.

IR codes

Learn: point the remote at the end of the switch, 30cm–1m away, and press its button. A sound is said as the code is sent.
